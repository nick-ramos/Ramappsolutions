Revolving utilization

After you pay

  • Do not close the paid-off cards. Their open limits keep your utilization low and their age keeps your history long.
  • Pay before the statement closes. Bureaus see the statement balance, not the due-date balance. A paid card that still reports a high statement balance looks maxed out.
  • Expect the update in 30 to 45 days. Utilization has no memory. Once a low balance reports, the score responds that cycle.
  • Hold off on new credit for 6 months. A new inquiry and a new account would blunt the gain you just bought.
  • Keep installment loans paying on time. Payment history is 35% of the score. One late payment costs more than any payoff earns.

Weighting follows FICO's published model: payment history 35%, amounts owed 30%, length of history 15%, credit mix 10%, new credit 10%. Score bands are estimates, not a promise. Interest figures use simple annual interest on the current balance.
