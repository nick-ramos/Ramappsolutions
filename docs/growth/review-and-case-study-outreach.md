## Text / email: case study ask (for clients who already love it)
Hi [Name], your project is one of my best examples of what this work can do.
Could I turn it into a short case study for my site? I'd write it up and send
it to you to approve first. All I need is about 10 minutes to ask a few
questions:
1. What was the problem before?
2. How much time / how many leads / how much money was it costing you?
3. What's different now? (a number is ideal)
4. Anything you'd tell another owner thinking about it?

## Case study format (keep it to 150 words)
- Before: one sentence, with the pain in numbers if possible
- What I built: one sentence
- After: the measurable result
- Quote: one line from the client

Never publish a number the client has not confirmed.
